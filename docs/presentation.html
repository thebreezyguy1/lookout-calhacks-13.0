Devpost pitch (2–3 sentences)

Scams against older adults don't look suspicious one transaction at a time; they look suspicious as a pattern. Lookout watches a parent's accounts and credit file with their consent, spots scam patterns like gift cards bought at three stores in one afternoon or a new credit card on a file that's been quiet for years, and responds in proportion: a gentle check-in with the parent, an alert to family with the evidence, or a recommended hold that a person must approve. It never moves money, and every decision is explained and audited.

A caller says he's her grandson and he's in jail. Each purchase on its own looks like ordinary shopping. Together they are the most common scam payment pattern there is. Mom is Dolores, a real CRS sandbox persona: 74, retired, every account closed for years.

How it works

Rules you can audit, Claude for judgment, a policy in charge

DataNessie bank activity (read-only), CRS credit reports from 3 bureaus plus Fraud Finder, and a labeled scenario injector for the demo.
Rules16 transaction rules and 10 identity signals, combined with a noisy-OR so weak signals add up and one strong signal is enough.
ClaudeNames the pattern, writes the family summary and Mom's check-in. Returns JSON through a forced tool call, validated with Pydantic.
Policy + auditPicks the tier. Every decision is logged with the events, rule hits, reasoning, confidence and tier.

Claude can raise the risk but can only lower the rules' score by 0.15 at most, so a strong signal can't be argued away. If Claude fails or returns invalid output, the rules result stands.

Where the rules fall short (holdout)

  • Missed Gift cards labeled only "Apple Store", with no "gift card" wording.
  • False alarm A car loan on an active credit file: two inquiries and a new account.
  • False alarm A $48,250 closing wire to a title company she'd already paid earnest money to.
  • Two normals got a gentle check-in, not an alarm: a $300 wedding gift card and a monthly Western Union to her sister.

Why that's the right story

Every miss needs context a keyword can't see: a relationship in her history, or the meaning of a merchant. That's the job of the Claude layer, and the next eval to run is the same holdout with Claude on (run_evals --set holdout --llm).

The identity check doesn't treat credit quality as fraud: bankruptcy, collections and a low score stay clear, because that's the person's own history.

Responsible design

Built for her dignity as much as her money

Consent and visibility

Mom opts in, and her screen shows her the exact activity Lookout is asking about. It never shames, and never asks for passwords, codes or account numbers.

Read-only, always

No code path creates a transfer, purchase or withdrawal for her. Holds and freezes are recommendations a person approves.

Explainable

Each decision carries the events, the rule hits with weights, Claude's reasoning, confidence and tier, in an audit log the family can read.

Few false alarms

Low confidence caps the response at a check-in. Normal life (gifts, travel, the HOA) is part of the eval set on purpose.
